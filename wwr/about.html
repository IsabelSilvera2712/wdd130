Our mission is to create safe, memorable river adventures that bring people closer to nature and each other.

History

Founded in 2010 by a group of river-loving friends, our company has grown from one small rafting crew into a team dedicated to sharing unforgettable river adventures. Wild Current Rafting began with a small crew of river guides who wanted to share their love of white water and the outdoors. They started by leading local trips and helping guests discover the excitement of paddling through the rapids.

Over the years, Wild Current Rafting has grown into a team of experienced guides serving families, friends, and adventure seekers. We remain committed to offering safe, memorable river trips and welcoming everyone to enjoy the beauty of the water.
